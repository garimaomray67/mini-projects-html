01 — The Mountains

A world above the clouds.

The Himalayas are a vast mountain range stretching across several countries in Asia. Their name comes from Sanskrit words meaning "abode of snow."

From peaceful valleys and ancient monasteries to towering snow-covered peaks, the Himalayas are a place where nature and culture exist side by side.

The mountains have shaped the lives, traditions and landscapes of millions of people for thousands of years.

04 — People & Culture

Where ancient traditions live.

The Himalayas are home to diverse communities with rich traditions, languages, art and spiritual practices.

Buddhist monasteries, colourful prayer flags, mountain villages and centuries-old festivals form an important part of Himalayan life.
